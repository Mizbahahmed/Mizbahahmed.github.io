01 / PROFILE

From field data to meaningful maps.

My PhD focuses on crop monitoring with multispectral and hyperspectral data and applications of machine learning and deep learning. My main research project investigates water quality across local, regional, and global scales, including measurements from an ASD spectrometer aboard a research vessel.

I previously worked on Louisiana coastal vegetation monitoring using UAV multispectral and LiDAR data, drought modeling, and GIS projects. I’m interested in research and applied roles in AgTech, environmental monitoring, UAV systems, and geospatial technology.
